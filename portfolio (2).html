About

I'm a university student and self-taught mobile developer from Egypt. I started with Flutter, shipped my first game to Google Play, and kept going — learning release builds, store policy, and monetization the hard way, one rejection at a time.

I build two kinds of things: party games in Arabic that friends actually play in the same room, and utility apps that solve a problem I ran into myself.

I'm comfortable going past the Flutter layer when a project needs it — writing native Kotlin for Android APIs, wiring up Firebase, or reading the platform docs until something clicks.
